Soulie, an emotional wellness product for children and parents

Children ages 4 to 10 often feel more than they can name. Soulie helps them understand and express emotions, and helps parents respond with more empathy and confidence.

  • Defined primary and secondary users, product goals, core user journeys, and the MVP feature set.
  • Wrote the product requirements document: feature architecture, interaction principles, emotional check-ins, self-guided support tools, growth journaling, parent-facing insights, and privacy considerations.
  • Used AI-assisted development tools, including OpenAI Codex, to turn requirements into prototype functionality, then refined flows and features over several iterations.
  • Balanced child usability, developmental appropriateness, caregiver needs, and user agency, drawing on my early childhood education background.

A restaurant recommender that uses your social graph

Ratings and reviews are generic, and choosing where to eat wears people out. Our team treated restaurant discovery as a personalized ranking problem, using social connections, preferences, location, time, and behavior.

  • Contributed to a hybrid approach that combines LightGCN embeddings, Jaccard-weighted social influence, candidate filtering, and feature-driven ranking on the Yelp Open Dataset.
  • Developed and evaluated an interactive prototype with recommendation explanations, social-graph visualization, score breakdowns, and location-based discovery, deployed as a React and Vite application.
  • Evaluated recommendations on held-out interactions and compared them against a popularity baseline.
